01Data stored by the app

Rafik Al Mouslim works primarily offline. Your language, theme, reading mode, text size and presentation preferences, together with your favorites, last reading position, reading positions and adhkar progress, are stored in the app’s private space on your device.

The app does not require a user account and does not synchronize this information with a developer-operated server. The developer does not receive your favorites, progress, searches or reading choices. The app does not add these local data to advertising requests.

02Location, prayer times and qibla

With your Android permission, the app can use your precise or approximate foreground location to configure prayer times and qibla. You can deny or revoke this permission and use the city choices provided by the app.

The selected coordinates, city name, time zone and calculation method are retained locally for later calculations. The app does not request background location and does not continuously track you.

To obtain a city name, the app may occasionally query Android’s geocoding service. Depending on the device, that service may transmit the request and coordinates to its own provider over the Internet under that provider’s policy. The app does not add your location to AdMob requests.

03Backup and changing devices

Android automatic backup, cloud restore and automatic device-to-device transfer are disabled in the Rafik Al Mouslim configuration. The app has no developer-operated synchronization or backup service.

A normal app update keeps the data already present on the device. However, clearing the app’s storage, uninstalling it or changing phones deletes or does not automatically transfer preferences, favorites, location and progress. The developer cannot recover them remotely.

04Advertising and Google services

Rafik Al Mouslim uses Google AdMob to show banners on selected screens that are not dedicated to religious reading, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices.

Google states that its advertising SDK automatically collects and shares technical data, including the IP address that may indicate a general area, app or ad interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers, including the advertising ID when available. These data may be used to deliver and measure ads, analyse their operation and prevent fraud.

UMP processes the information needed to present and record your consent choices. These Google activities are separate from the favorites, progress, religious content and location stored locally by the app. They vary according to your choices, device settings and advertising configuration.

05Permissions and choices

You can deny or revoke location and notification permissions in Android settings. Prayer reminders are optional, scheduled locally and may require the special exact-alarm permission. The app can reschedule them after a restart or time change when you have enabled them.

When a consent form is required, UMP presents it in the app. The advertising privacy option lets you revisit the available choices when required. You can also manage or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Declining personalization does not necessarily stop all technical data processing for permitted ads.

06Retention and deletion

Configured preferences, favorites, progress and location data remain on your device for as long as you keep the app and its storage. To delete all local Rafik Al Mouslim data, clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

The developer cannot access, restore or remotely delete these local data. Google and other providers you choose apply their own retention periods and deletion methods, as described in their policies.

07Data protection

Local data are stored in the app’s private space, protected by Android access controls. The app blocks cleartext HTTP traffic. Communications required for advertising services and geocoding use the secure mechanisms provided by Android and their service providers.

No technical measure can guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your phone and install available security updates.

08Contact and privacy requests

To contact the developer of Rafik Al Mouslim about this policy or exercise rights that apply to your situation, email capitech92@gmail.com.

If you write to us, your email address and message are processed to respond to your request and kept as long as needed to follow it up, subject to legal obligations. Do not send sensitive data unless necessary.

09Visiting this website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no ads, audience analytics, forms, scripts or cookies added by the developer. GitHub receives technical information needed to host the site, including IP addresses and usage data, and may keep logs to provide and secure its service. Its privacy statement is linked below.

External websites opened from this page apply their own policies.

10Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if features, SDKs or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version. The French, English and Arabic versions describe the same app and practices.
